Privacy Policy

IMPOSTER is a pass-and-play party word game. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app and its word generation features.

Information we do not require

IMPOSTER does not require an account, password, profile, payment method, contact list, camera access, microphone access, or precise location to play.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected language on your device so it can remember that setting when you reopen the app. Player names, category choices, difficulty, timer settings, imposter settings, and current round details are used for local gameplay. The app does not create an account for you.

Round generation requests

Some rounds use a server-side generation service to create movie or celebrity words or to translate a static word and clue. These requests can include the selected language, category, difficulty, player count, source word and clue for translation, and recently played generated words. The app sends player count, not player names, for round generation.

Technical information

The backend and service providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, user agent, app version, request time, and diagnostic logs. The backend uses a hashed request key for rate limiting round generation requests.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payments.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the game, generate or translate round content, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, maintain security, and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Children

IMPOSTER is designed as a group party game and is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact support so it can be reviewed.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the current version took effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support options listed on the support page.
