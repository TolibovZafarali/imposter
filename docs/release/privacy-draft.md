## Information stored on your device

IMPOSTER remembers your selected word language. It also stores when this gameplay history began, completed-round counts, recent round identifiers, and the timing of ad and review-request attempts. These local records keep interruptions infrequent and prevent the same completion from being counted twice. They are not an account or a public profile. Player names are used for gameplay and are not sent in round-generation requests or provided by the app as advertising targeting information.

## Advertising in ad-supported releases

Ad-supported iOS releases use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to present occasional ads between completed rounds and manage privacy choices. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, product interactions, crash diagnostics and performance information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Data handling depends on your choices, applicable region and the final ad configuration. Requests from the app ask for non-personalized ads; this does not mean the advertising service processes no information.

Use Ad privacy choices in Settings to review available consent options. Where tracking permission is needed, the system asks for it; declining does not prevent gameplay. Apple permission and regional consent are separate choices. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's partner information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Ratings

The app may ask the App Store to show its native review dialog after repeated completed play. Apple decides whether to display the request and handles any rating or review you submit. IMPOSTER does not receive the rating you choose through this request and does not reward positive reviews.
